# Civil Plan of a 1200 SFT flat using AutoCAD 2027
Title: Design a civil plan with electrical fittings and fixtures according to BNBC using AutoCAD.

Exp 1:	A Civil Plan of a flat layout covering 1200 SFT (approx.) using AutoCAD 2027 software.

Exp 2: The Fittings for the civil plan, including electrical fittings such as lights, fans, and various sockets, as per BNBC.

The planning and design of a residential building require careful consideration of both its architectural layout and electrical services to ensure comfort, functionality, safety, and efficient use of space. In this practical exercise, a residential flat of approximately 1200 square feet (SFT) has been designed using AutoCAD, followed by the preparation of its electrical fitting layout in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC).
Experiment 1 focuses on the preparation of the Civil Plan of the flat. The layout includes essential residential spaces such as bedrooms, drawing and dining areas, kitchen, toilets, balcony, lobby, lift, and stair facilities. The arrangement and dimensions of the rooms have been planned to provide proper circulation, natural ventilation, accessibility, and functional use of the available floor area.
Experiment 2 involves developing the Electrical Fitting Plan based on the prepared civil layout. The drawing indicates the locations of lights, ceiling fans, wall lights, switches, sockets, TV points, exhaust fans, circuit breakers, and other electrical outlets required for normal residential use. The fittings are distributed according to the function and size of each room, with attention to convenience and electrical safety.
Overall, these two exercises demonstrate how to integrate an architectural floor plan with an electrical service layout using AutoCAD. The work also provides a practical understanding of residential planning, electrical fitting arrangements, and the application of BNBC requirements in building design.
